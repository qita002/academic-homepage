Research

Fei Ding works at the intersection of quantum magnetic materials and magnetoelectrochemistry, combining experiment and theory to control and understand magnetic states. His work uses crystal growth, magnetometry and electrochemical modulation as experimental tools, combined with first-principles calculation, to reveal magnetic ground-state evolution under ionic/charge modulation and its applications in magnetic storage and energy-storage mechanisms.
Core skill set: crystal growth + magnetic measurement + electrochemical modulation + DFT.
